About CEGX

Over 15 years of procurement expertise, trusted by government bodies, NHS trusts, and leading enterprises across the United Kingdom.

🏛
Our Story

Built on Expertise, Driven by Results

Founded in 2009, CEGX began with a clear mission: to bring private sector procurement excellence to the public sector. What started as a small consultancy has grown into one of the UK's most trusted procurement and supply chain agencies.

Today, we manage over £500 million in contracts annually, serving government departments, NHS trusts, local authorities, defence agencies, and FTSE-listed enterprises. Our team of 40+ specialists brings deep category knowledge across every major procurement vertical.

We believe that smart procurement isn't just about cost savings — it's about creating sustainable value, driving innovation, and building resilient supply chains that serve communities and organisations for the long term.
